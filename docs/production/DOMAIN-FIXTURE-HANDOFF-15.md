## Pack meaning, applicability and updates

Model pack identity/namespace/version and content digest; supported jurisdiction,
activity/entity scope; authoritative source references with publication/effective/
retrieval dates; interpretation ID/version; eligible accountable reviewer; obligation
definitions; control/evaluation mappings; review/expiry policy and dependency profile.
Source provenance, reviewer interpretation, implementation status and observed control
effectiveness remain separate. Do not output a blanket compliance badge.

Applicability is an explicit typed predicate over authorized scope facts, with
applicable/not-applicable/unknown/conflict states. Unknown geography/activity or
missing required source is not global applicability and not an automatic exemption.
A not-applicable decision needs authorized rationale and evidence; it cannot hide
a mandatory baseline obligation. Expiry uses a bound clock and [effective, expires)
validity, not eventual cleanup scheduling. Record effective and recorded times.

Synthetic pack tests use FICTIONAL-J-A and invented obligations expressly marked as
test data. Real packs require their own maintained source provenance and qualified
accountable interpretation before offering jurisdiction-specific assurance. Ordinary
software/public-service workflows must not depend on the financial reference pack.
Generic scoped authority/retention still apply even when no industry pack is selected.

An update stages a new version, compares applicable obligations and previews affected
controls/processes/evals/approvals/active runs. Reviewed activation commits the new
assignment and authoritative invalidation together; old decision/source bindings
remain immutable. Explicit approved transition periods need owners and expiry, not
an indefinite grandfathered grant. Conflicting obligations route arbitration without
an LLM choosing a compliant answer. Retention conflicts are scoped policy decisions,
not automatic prioritization inferred from a pack title.

## Simulation, cases and business continuity

Simulation reuses the compiled workflow semantics in effect-free mode, with a pinned
model/interpreter, virtual clock, random algorithm/version/seed, input order, horizon,
units, capacity calendar, service/arrival distributions and calibration provenance.
No secret/effect capability is issued to simulated actions. Store assumptions and
simulated results separately from observations; reproducibility is conditioned on
all pinned inputs, not the seed alone. Reusing a simulation as a forecast must retain
its scope, uncertainty and calibration limits.

AC1 is deliberately deterministic: twelve cases arrive at time zero, the agent stage
has sufficient capacity and zero modeled delay, each manual worker completes eight
cases within the one-hour horizon. Observe completions at the horizon inclusively:
one worker leaves four queued; two leave zero. No stochastic arrival/queueing theorem
is implied. A materialized fixture must define ties, completion timestamps, initial
queue and capacity units so agents cannot invent incompatible numeric expectations.

Reject probability outside [0,1], NaN/infinity, negative durations/capacity, incompatible
units and invalid distributions before numeric output. A valid profile without
empirical calibration may simulate but remains unvalidated. An invalid input has no
result at all; it must not be relabeled merely unvalidated. Missing empirical data
cannot pass measured-business readiness. Validate any conversions against a pinned
unit contract; do not invent currency/time/calibration transformations.

Actual exceptions/disputes are separate cases: affected commitment/revision, claimant,
observed partial result, evidence, applicable versioned playbook, decision owner,
eligibility, deadlines and remedy options. A simulated favorable remedy does not
authorize the real one. Compensation needs current approval and its own effect
identity/receipt; original fulfilment and residual obligations remain visible.
Observed forecast error proposes calibration/resource/process change, not automatic
staffing, spend or autonomy expansion. Reuse the common change protocol.

Continuity plans link critical services, people/suppliers/resources, detection evidence,
impact scope, substitute eligibility, capacity and authority, handover acceptance,
temporary grant bounds, business recovery objectives and return-to-primary criteria.
Names or job titles are insufficient proof of qualification. No eligible substitute
means paused/escalated work with an owner, not an automatically assigned fallback.
Simultaneous failures must evaluate capacity shared with existing commitments.

At transfer, recheck current grant/qualification and commitment generation, fence old
dispatch and commit new assignment/accepted handover/result/outbox together. External
supplier changes are not atomically reversible: record each provider effect and
reconcile already-accepted work. A temporary principal cannot approve its own grant
or use another account to bypass entity scope, quorum or immutable protection.
Review X-07 (supplier failure plus absent approver in another legal unit) explicitly.

Return to primary is another reviewed transition. Unknown substitute fulfilment blocks
duplicate primary fulfilment. Temporary grants expire or are revoked on handback;
old sessions/leases stay fenced after restart. Preserve commitment identities and
assignment history. Business objectives include time to minimum acceptable service,
capacity, backlog and critical obligations. Infrastructure recovery is separate: a
30-minute database restore may pass while two-hour service recovery against a one-hour
objective fails. Both results and remaining accountable remediation must be visible.

## Qualification is an evidence product, not a boolean endpoint

An assessment owns candidate digest, deployment/support profile, required obligation
manifest, tests/fixtures/versions, actual run IDs, raw artifacts, reviewer identity and
independence, findings, applicability/exclusion decisions, timestamps and disposition.
The reviewer checks evidence produced independently of expected comparator values.
Missing, failed, blocked, not-run and stale evidence are distinguishable. A signed
manifest establishes integrity under a trust policy, not completeness or truth.

Keep required inventories outside the candidate's unilateral control. Reject omission,
duplicate identity, mismatched revision/scope/provider or stale applicability rather
than counting uploaded receipts. Where reviewed reuse across revisions is allowed,
record exact unchanged artifact/dependency coverage and rationale; no silent bulk reuse.
Every high/critical security finding requires remediation and independent retest on
the candidate. Do not downgrade severity to pass. Medium findings still have owned
dispositions under the release policy. Never impersonate an independent reviewer.

Security review covers all PC boundaries and relevant F/X combinations, including
UI/API, workers, modules, ingress, effects, projections, exports and extension isolation.
Run positive authorized controls alongside attacks so a broken endpoint cannot pass
security by rejecting everyone. Test identities are scoped isolated fixtures; use
synthetic secret canaries that unauthorized models/actors never need to receive.
Capture raw evidence with restricted access, scan/redact before publishing and fail
the test if a canary reached a forbidden sink. A scrubbed public report cannot erase
the fact that the original runtime leaked the canary.

Restore must reconcile current revocations, key status, cell/worker epochs and accepted
remote effects before enabling work. If current security state cannot be recovered,
remain restricted and obtain authorized recovery, not trust old backup grants.
Routine retirement and key compromise differ; retain historical public trust metadata
and disclose as-of limits. Holds prevent prohibited deletion, not authorized access
revocation. Search/projections/export/download paths must enforce current permissions
even while their content revisions lag. Check X-01/X-05/X-08 at their real boundaries.

## Extension safety, structural change and exit

Extension certification binds immutable package and transitive dependency digests,
namespace/schema/predicate/action versions, host/module/interpreter compatibility,
requested capability set, UI/accessibility, migration/readback, classification,
retention and independent evidence. Keep submitted/tested/certified/installed/enabled/
revoked states separate. Certification and signature do not grant customer authority.
Declarative data and untrusted code are distinct: code runs only in qualified isolated
adapters with brokered capability, never inside the control-plane process or an
unrestricted UI plugin. Namespace ownership and core schemas cannot be shadowed.

Core authority/identity/deny precedence cannot be changed by a pack. An RFC is an
explicit architecture-change process, not a bypass code. Requests for undeclared
capabilities fail even for a correctly signed package. Dependency cycles, omitted
transitive code and compatibility gaps block certification. Initial install with no
prior schema is distinct from a no-op migration; migrations preserve typed historical
interpretation. A revoked package pauses new dependent work immediately, with actual
already-accepted effects retained for reconciliation.

Rollback must be trusted and data-compatible now. Do not execute revoked old code to
read data merely because it was once installed. Preserve unknown namespaced fields
as retained opaque evidence if current interpretation is unsupported; never silently
drop them. A supported inert read/export mode is not evidence that the old workflow
can run. Removal inventories live definitions, instances, evidence and exports;
migrate/retire active references before code uninstall, retain schema descriptors
needed for interpretation and disclose unsupported behavior/losses explicitly.

Reorganisation maps old IDs to continuing/new identities, effective legal scope,
accountable commitments, source authority and provenance with explicit acceptance.
Tenant boundary is independent: a legal merger or chart move cannot combine tenant
data or grant cross-tenant access. Continuing objects keep identity; a genuine split
creates scoped successor IDs with lineage rather than duplicating one identity into
two writable owners. Same-named parties remain separate unless reviewed identity
equivalence is evidenced. Commitment transfer requires authority/acceptance; a UI
mapping cannot itself novate a real external agreement.

Retirement request first computes dependencies: active workflow/runtime history,
unknown effects, commitments, holds, imports/exports, extension schemas, schedules
and provider credentials. Block destructive removal or enter an owned drain plan.
No automatic cascade deletes legal/operating obligations. Every disable/revoke action
has its own scoped authority. Reconciliation and hold-preserving access may remain
under narrow grants without leaving normal business scheduling active.

Portable bundles bind one snapshot, chunks/hashes, typed schema/version descriptors,
identity/provenance maps, scope/classification/retention and explicit unsupported
content. Resume by manifest identity/cursor, never add current-head records halfway
through an old export. Verify every chunk/reference and actual usable independent
import, not merely that a ZIP opens. Import stages data and inert grant/connector/
schedule descriptions; no secrets, sessions or past approval becomes live. Destination
scope/mapping and effect authority require fresh customer decisions.

Exit has separate export-verified, normal-dispatch-disabled, remote-schedules-confirmed,
retention-resolved and disposal-outcome states. Provider outage on even one owned
schedule keeps cessation unknown and prevents completed exit. An OrgWard database row
marked disabled is insufficient proof of provider-side shutdown. Retained held records
need reason, scope, access, owner and review/disposal date; deleted content needs actual
store/projection/export-cache results. Already delivered customer copies cannot be
recalled by deleting the source. Final closure reports that limit rather than claiming
universal erasure. All actions are sandbox fixtures until separately authorized.
