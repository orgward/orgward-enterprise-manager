# Adapt, exercise, qualify and leave — enterprise user journeys

Required future behavior for T-100–T-108, not implemented screens or evidence of
production readiness. [Implementation handoff](../production/DOMAIN-FIXTURE-HANDOFF-15.md)
contains the contract decisions and independent evidence obligations.

## Select a domain pack without inventing compliance

The accountable owner opens **Requirements → Domain packs**, selects a supported
version and reviews activity/entity/jurisdiction scope, exact sources, interpreted
obligations, control/evaluation links and expiry. An eligible reviewer accepts the
interpretation before scoped activation. The screen separately shows interpretation,
configured control and measured effectiveness—never one universal compliance tick.

Unsupported scope, stale source or a retention/deletion conflict creates an owned
blocker. An agent may propose research or an interpretation; it cannot invent legal
certainty. Updating the pack shows affected processes, controls, evaluations and
active work, with separate review before activation. Previous decisions retain their
old source/version. A software business or public library without the financial
pack can still run generic workflows without bank-only mandatory fields.

## Ask “what if?” and handle what actually happened

The process owner opens a service process and chooses **Simulate staffing**.
Diagram and structured form expose the same activities, queues, calendars, human/
agent capacity, exception branches and assumptions. A deterministic example shows
twelve arrivals and capacity eight: four remain queued with one worker, zero with
two. The result is clearly simulated; changing the slider does not hire anyone,
spend money or prove real service capacity.

Invalid units/probabilities identify the offending field and produce no numeric
result. Valid but uncalibrated inputs can simulate with an unvalidated label and
an owned evidence gap. Comparing actual observations with forecast creates a reviewable
resource/process correction. Acceptance and effect authority are separate actions.

A customer dispute opens a real case linked to the commitment and partial fulfilment.
The worker follows a pinned applicable playbook, requests the required remedy decision
and sees separately evidenced compensation. The system retains “two of three units
delivered”; a replacement action cannot erase the initial partial failure. Editing
the playbook uses the shared versioned workflow editor and cannot remove mandatory
authority controls by renaming or moving a step.

## Lose a key person and supplier, then recover

An operator declares both dependencies unavailable. The continuity view shows which
critical work is affected, eligible substitutes, remaining capacity, required handover
and unresolved gaps. Qualified person B can receive a scoped task after acceptance;
an unqualified supplier cannot receive work merely because its name is on a fallback
list. Without an approver, protected work pauses and escalates to a named route.

Temporary rights are narrow and expiring. A fallback account cannot bypass legal-unit
scope or approve its own emergency grant. When the primary supplier returns, the
operator reconciles replacement commitments and unknown effects before handback.
Reopening the original queue must not order the same service twice. Old assignments
stay in history while temporary dispatch rights are revoked.

**Exercise results** show business and platform recovery separately. For example,
the platform restored in 30 minutes, but service took two hours against a one-hour
objective and still has inadequate capacity. The business drill failed despite a
successful infrastructure restore, and remediation remains owned.

## Qualify a release with actual evidence

The release reviewer opens **Readiness → Supported release**, with candidate digest,
deployment/module/provider profile, required obligations and linked raw evidence.
The view shows not-run, failed, blocked, stale and passed distinctly. Security review
lists findings and independent retests. Load results show actual workload, sample
counts, errors, cold/warm compilation, soak duration and measured limits—not only
a green average. A missing test is not a passed test with zero failures.

An independent customer operator must install, configure identity, back up, restore,
upgrade and diagnose using the shipped documentation and customer credentials.
Undocumented developer rescue becomes a finding. Independent users perform supported
role journeys across two non-bank businesses and the financial reference, including
keyboard/screen-reader use. Completion percentages cannot excuse a critical confusion
about whether an action is authorized or a business is ready.

For the integrated SDLC journey, the reviewer follows business design → accepted
claims/Sentinel snapshot → policy/decision → bounded agent changes → evaluated
artifact → independently approved deployment → observed outcome/corrective proposal.
Change the policy or artifact after evaluation: the intended boundary must block,
with a usable repair path. Correct it, create fresh context/evaluation/approval and
resume once; old evidence remains historical. Both the original and user-edited
workflow profiles must have their required actual execution evidence.

Passing the initial P/E gates alone cannot satisfy missing portfolio obligations.
T-106 qualifies its preserved portfolio baseline; T-132 still owns the later full
SaaS/migration endpoint. These assessment screens and tests are future implementation
requirements, not functionality delivered by this checkpoint.

## Add, upgrade or remove an extension

An extension author submits a versioned namespace, typed concepts/relations/forms,
adapter capabilities, schemas, dependencies and migration. The compatibility lab
tests exact package bytes, UI accessibility, permissions, data migration and isolation.
Certification does not install or activate the extension for a customer automatically.
The customer separately grants the minimal needed capabilities under current policy.

A signed extension that attempts to replace core authority rules or request undeclared
network access is rejected. A failed upgrade or revoked signature pauses affected work;
recovery uses a currently trusted compatible version or remains visibly blocked.
Unknown fields and old evidence are retained, not silently dropped to make downgrade
look successful. Inert exported data is not presented as executable functionality.

**Remove extension** first lists referenced processes, fields, active instances and
retained evidence. The owner must migrate or explicitly retire active dependencies.
Export discloses unsupported interpretation and preserves the namespaced data. A
button labeled uninstall is not permission to destroy obligations or history.

## Reorganize or exit without losing responsibilities

An architect proposes a business-unit merger or split, reviews identity/source maps,
effective scope, owners and commitments, then publishes an accepted successor design.
Same-named customers are not automatically merged. A genuine split creates linked
successor identities where necessary; it does not make two independent writers own
the same object. Legal structure and tenant security boundaries remain separate.

For exit, the administrator sees export verification, drain/reconciliation, external
schedule cessation and retention/disposal as separate phases. A customer independently
verifies and imports the portable model/evidence. Interrupted transfer resumes the
same immutable snapshot. Imported grants, connectors and schedules remain disabled
until the destination separately authorizes them; credentials/sessions are not copied.

If one external scheduler cannot be reached, its state is unknown and exit cannot
be marked complete. Held evidence shows reason, permitted access, owner and later
review/disposal instead of “deleted.” Already delivered copies cannot be recalled by
deleting their source. The final outcome explicitly states what was exported, stopped,
retained, deleted or still unresolved.

## Interaction requirements shared by every journey

Retain authorized workspace/baseline/selection across map, list and detail views.
Forms and diagram edits invoke the same typed commands and version checks. Show
loading, successful empty, stale, denied, conflict, failed and recovery states; keep
permitted drafts and offer explicit compare/rebase. No blind action replay after
reconnect, no leaking hidden objects through search or impact counts, and no inspector
description counted as a working action. Required tests inspect actual stored results,
authority decisions, provider effects and recovery—not only screen labels.
