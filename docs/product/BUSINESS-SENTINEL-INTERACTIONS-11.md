# Business operation to Sentinel to SDLC — interaction scenarios 11

Required future behavior, not a report of working screens. Continues the
[SDLC walkthrough](SDLC-INTERACTIONS-09.md) and the hard
[configurable-workflow contract](CONFIGURABLE-WORKFLOWS.md).
Concrete original acceptance expectations: T-56–T-67 in
`contracts/enterprise/domain-vectors-11.mjs`. These do not replace ES/PQ/SQ journeys.

## 1. Design the promise, not just the process diagram

A repair-business owner opens the offering from the customer journey. They see
the need, request/collection/repair/return steps, accountable roles, capacity,
cost assumptions and success measure. In the agreement inspector they record
four chairs due by 1 October and a reciprocal USD 400 payment obligation.
Contract source, interpretation status and actual performance are separate tabs.
Saving an agreement never sends money or declares it legally enforceable.

The owner changes a due date from either the commitment form or process lens.
Both edits address the same canonical commitment and use the same preview/review
path. Impact lists affected capacity windows, customer promises, reminders and
workflow inputs. Future-effective terms do not rewrite past performance. If two
chairs have already been returned, a reviewed cure concerns the remaining two;
the original promise, receipt and dispute remain inspectable.

Missing owner/performer, incompatible units, unsourced currency conversion and
unknown interpretation have distinct repair actions. A visually connected map
can be saved as a draft but cannot become an enabled process merely by looking
complete. Accounting references identify their external system and posting;
governance Ledger events are not monetary postings.

## 2. Staff and perform real work, including manual and physical work

The process owner sees a supplier outage against scheduled work. OrgWard proposes
a substitute only after comparing skill, availability, location/calendar and
scope. Proposal is not assignment or a capability grant. An eligible owner
approves it, creating a reservation with a concurrency-safe capacity check.
Two simultaneous six-hour requests against eight hard-limit hours cannot both
succeed. Explicit soft overbooking shows the excess and approving decision.

A technician receives a work-order form, reserves a repair bench, records the
actual handover and releases capacity. No repository or code generation is
required. Departure or cancellation releases reservations once and leaves an
acknowledged successor or visible unowned-work escalation. Availability does
not expose a colleague's restricted personnel reason.

For ten promised chairs, seven delivered with two damaged means five accepted
and five outstanding—not “70% complete, success.” The owner opens a discrepancy,
selects a reviewed replacement/compensation route and tracks the new evidence.
An uncertain external remedy stays under reconciliation. Neither human attestation
nor a sensor reading silently becomes legal certification. Unqualified machinery
actions remain unsupported even if an editor renames or rearranges their nodes.

## 3. Adapt the workflow without losing its controls

The owner opens the repair workflow's Diagram or Structured editor. They add a
damage-inspection substep, branch on a typed acceptance result and add a bounded
rework loop with an escalation exit. Both editors save the same node identities,
criteria and action references. Simulation uses fixture outcomes and has no real
effects. Publication, enablement and starting work remain separate commands.

Bulk reassignment previews each affected role, control and workflow node. The
user chooses all-or-nothing or explicit partial mode before reviewing the digest.
Partial mode returns exact applied/rejected rows; it never applies hidden rows.
Current authority is rechecked at submit. A lost response leads to command-status
reconciliation, not a new anonymous batch. Draft content still authorized for the
user survives reconnect; revoked restricted data must not remain freshly readable.

Running work keeps its pinned definition. A new owner field in the design does
not silently transfer an active approval or rerun a completed handover. Eligible
in-flight migration follows the explicit pause, state mapping, effect reconciliation,
independent review and successor-run protocol. Resolving a discussion comment
does not approve the workflow or resolve an integrity finding.

## 4. Measure the business separately from the software

The owner compares two staffing/pricing alternatives using declared demand,
capacity, unit cost, currency, formula version and uncertainty. A scenario can
predict USD 200 versus USD 220 margin without writing either into actual revenue.
Changed assumptions create a new experiment; old results remain reproducible.

After deployment, the service can be technically healthy while only six of ten
returns meet a nine-of-ten target. The outcome view creates a proposed correction,
not a silent budget or policy adjustment. Readiness shows design facets, enabled
work, control evidence freshness and measured outcomes separately. Product P/E
qualification appears under installation with its own unchanged denominators.
Excluding a critical dependency requires the actual scoped decision; even an
allowed risk acceptance cannot manufacture a passed test or override a mandatory deny.

## 5. Bring existing knowledge into Sentinel safely

An architect selects an authorized repository revision and allowlisted paths.
The source workbench lists Markdown, YAML and Mermaid ingestion, exclusions and
failures with exact coverage. A four-file source containing three supported files
and an unsupported image shows four dispositions, not “100% ingested.” Malicious
paths, symlinks, archive expansion and oversized files are blocked before unsafe
extraction. A source outage is incomplete coverage, not deletion of accepted truth.

Extraction proposes an AUTHORITY claim from the exact ADR revision/path with a
bounded redacted excerpt, extractor version and confidence. Two possible identity
matches remain alternatives. Instructions inside the document cannot invoke tools,
merge identities or accept the claim. Retrying a failed extraction consumes the
same bounded job budget and cannot leave a partially accepted claim set.

The reviewer inspects source, meaning, scope, conflicts and rationale, then accepts
or rejects using their actual session authority. Acceptance records an immutable
decision; the graph remains last-good/pending until the matching compile publishes.
Two conflicting accepted authorities remain visible inputs to a contradiction
finding. “Latest document wins” is not an authority rule. Closing a notification
does not fix the contradiction.

## 6. Follow a system change back into delivery

From a booking-service finding, the architect follows its interface/data-contract
consumers into onboarding, customer-intake capability and business objective.
A linked SDLC case pins those exact versions and the required independent contract
evaluations. One logical service can use several repositories and have separate
staging/production artifacts; those identities must not be flattened.

Renaming a physical email column preserves its semantic property ID through a
versioned mapping. Old observations remain interpretable. A material authority or
interface change invalidates affected evaluations and approvals atomically, before
the map catches up. An old release remains historical; a new protected effect
cannot use its stale permission. Sentinel supplies evidence and findings; it does
not execute the repair. SDLC, Arbiter, Warden and the shared workflow/effect path
retain their own responsibilities.

## UX and evidence required for every journey

Implement laptop map/list/form paths with keyboard equivalents, stable selection,
scope/version/freshness context, durable drafts, exact field errors and permitted
recovery actions. Verify loading, empty, stale, denied, conflict, failed and recovered
states. Read-only users inspect permitted records without receiving edit authority.
Evidence must join real UI actions to authenticated commands, persisted revisions,
outbox events, workflow history and independent effect observations. A screenshot,
example response or this walkthrough cannot satisfy that requirement.
