# Enterprise scenario and domain coverage catalogue

Revision 3. These 24 scenario contracts make enterprise work visible beyond software engineering. They are target workflows, not implemented ERP/CRM/HR/accounting products. Each maps to concrete tasks and the F-family/PC-boundary qualification strategy. Domain interpretations and external systems remain explicit rather than being invented by generation.

Support classes: **core** is required generic platform behavior; **model_and_review** requires usable design/analysis but does not claim automated real-world execution; **governed_adapter** requires a tested, authorized adapter and real isolated test effects; **qualified_domain_pack** requires reviewed domain semantics and certified integrations for the declared supported scope. Full-target completion requires every scenario's stated contract to pass; an unsupported live regulated action must fail safely and be labeled unsupported, not be falsely claimed supported. Broader domain automation requires an additional qualified pack release.

Every scenario carries: business trigger; initiating/accountable/approving actors; required object/context versions; inputs/outputs and sensitive data; role/authority separation; constraints and success measures; happy/denied/concurrent/partial/recovery states; evidence; and applicable pack/adapter/topology. Implement these as fixture manifests before claiming supported execution. A generic task runner is not enough unless the scenario's actual objects, UX and failure handling are verified.

Coverage includes strategy, commercial activity, operations, finance references, people, suppliers, assets, quality/risk, information, technology, customer service, governance and lifecycle. It does not promise to replace every specialist system of record. New enterprise-specific needs extend this catalogue with stable IDs, accountable interpretation and tests; they must not erase earlier stories or bypass the shared semantic/effect boundaries.
